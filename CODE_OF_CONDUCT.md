# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Detach community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Respect privacy and never share another person's credentials, private data, or security report.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
